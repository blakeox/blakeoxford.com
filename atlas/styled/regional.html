Sources, coverage and how to read the map

Scope. The 12 sovereign countries of South America and seven of Central America. Mexico and territories are not included in the country comparisons. The atlas is a historical snapshot, not a live election tracker. Shaded land represents places, not the number of voters.

Ideological blocs. National first-round presidential vote, grouped by the candidate or party’s published 1–20 ideology score: below 9 left, 9–13 center, above 13 right. Thresholds are atlas conventions. Uncoded votes are visible and not assigned to a bloc; shares use all candidates in the file. Source coverage varies by election and ends no later than 2018. Later candidate results are shown separately, without guessing ideology. The source combines expert scores with other classifications, including Wikipedia-based coding for some newer parties. Baker and Greene data and scoring notes · Baker and Greene (2011). Scoring inputs also include Coppedge (1998), Lodola and Queirolo (2005), Pop-Eleches (2009), and Wiesehomeier and Benoit (2009).

Presidential returns. Krause, Döring and Stoppe, PPEG 2025v1; historical national results from Baker and Greene where not supplied by PPEG; Argentina 2023 from Cámara Nacional Electoral. Latest means latest in this atlas, not in every country. Venezuela’s disputed 2024 presidential result is not shown. Carter Center observer statement. A first-round leader does not necessarily win. Runoff comparison never mixes first-round shares.

Legislative strongholds. CLEA lower chamber, release 18. Local party shares use the sum of reported, positive party/list tallies for that constituency (not seats or necessarily ballots). Repeated candidate rows with the same party total are counted once per normalized ballot label and area; conflicting totals are left out. Party alliances and label changes are not treated as the same party. Only first-round party tallies are used. Some systems report preference votes or overlapping tiers, so local shares are not summed to a countrywide vote. Cross-country map uses the median top-two gap in covered areas, not a national margin. Current admin boundaries from Natural Earth are shown only for matched places; old boundaries and electoral splits may differ.

Context overlays. World Bank WDI, 2023 resident population, recorded sex, urbanization, GDP per person in current U.S. dollars, sector share of GDP, and subsidies and other transfers as a share of expense where reported. This broad fiscal series is not household subsidy spending. Context is not evidence for how demographic groups voted. No individual voting histories are inferred.
