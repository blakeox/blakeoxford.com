Sources and methodology

Shares use votes cast for presidential candidates; blank and invalid ballots are excluded. Changes are November minus October in percentage points, computed before rounding. A gain in vote share does not identify how individual people voted. Nationwide totals include votes not separately mapped. The boundaries show the 24 domestic electoral districts; overseas votes and disputed territories are not mapped separately.
