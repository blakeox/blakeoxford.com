Sources and map limits

The local view aggregates the official DINE provisional presidential count from voting tables for October 22 and November 19. Shares exclude blank and invalid ballots; “gains” are percentage-point changes, not individual voter movements. The national atlas uses final district totals, so the two views need not sum to the same number.

Section outlines come from IGN-derived election geography. Circuit geometry is matched by province, section and circuit ID against 2025 or 2021 boundaries, not a definitive 2023 boundary survey. A matching ID does not guarantee an unchanged boundary. Unmatched circuits remain in the table and are not drawn. The map does not assign polling-table votes to street addresses or infer how individual residents voted.
