# Atlas homepage review

**Task:** Arrive on the Atlas, choose a map or an election, and find local detail.
**Captured before editing:** desktop 1440 × 1050 and mobile 390 × 844 in the existing Cloudflare development runtime using the production publication inputs.

1. **Land and learn:** The old homepage was the same page as the map. Scope was clear, but new visitors encountered multiple filters before choosing a task. The map began about 570 px down on a 390 px phone; the recent election archive appeared as a minor text link.
2. **Choose a path:** Region choices and country profiles were out of sequence. The workspace asked the visitor to choose variables, then presented a map and a rankings panel, before providing a clear starting point for reading a recent result or a country's history.
3. **Verify and drill down:** Keep the map's separate layers, transparent legends, exact country URLs and sources. Teach the distinction between geographic coverage and actual election availability rather than suggesting the map is a live tracker.

**Decision:** Make home an editorial entry point. Give map and election archive clear actions, make all country groups discoverable, surface the latest indexed results and methodology, and leave filters with the explorer. Retain the portfolio's existing fonts, colors, theme and global navigation. The map uses the Atlas's Natural Earth geometry rather than generated boundaries.

**Accessibility and limits:** Check keyboard navigation, mobile tap targets, long country labels, plain-text map alternative, and browser zoom/layout. Screenshots do not certify assistive-technology conformance or data completeness. Test all destinations and old map bookmarks separately before releasing.
