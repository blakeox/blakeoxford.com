Welcome to the newest corner of the site. I have been rebuilding my portfolio around three principles:

1. **Performance-first delivery.** Astro's island architecture lets me ship the minimum JavaScript needed for each page.
2. **Type-safe content.** Content collections and Zod schemas keep MDX frontmatter aligned with the UI, even as the project evolves.
3. **Automated quality gates.** Vitest, Playwright, Lighthouse CI, and mutation testing run on every branch, so regressions get caught before deployment.

Future posts will dive into the tooling behind my customer projects, the change management lessons that shaped them, and the experiments I'm running with AI-assisted operations. Stay tuned!
