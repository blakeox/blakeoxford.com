Fanalyx is my ongoing product build for finance teams that refuse to guess. I am rebuilding the entire experience around deterministic engines: typed schemas, reproducible math, and load-tested forecast pipelines. The same core powers dashboards, the API, and MCP connectors so every touchpoint surfaces identical numbers. We now ship personal finance modules alongside enterprise FP&A; families model debt payoff, runway, and savings with the same deterministic rigor.

The stack runs on Cloudflare Workers with persistent request IDs, giving us audit trails and coherent logs at edge speed. Ingestion flows pass through Zod-based validation before data ever touches the modeling engines, and every release is snapshot-tested against real customer load profiles.

Rather than bolt on opaque AI, I wrapped the deterministic core with explainable assist layers. Finance leads can ask for assumptions, scenario diffs, or recommended levers, and the responses are grounded in the stored calculations—not generative guesswork.

We treat operations like a product: self-healing Worker deployments, status dashboards, and 18 months of audit retention live out of the box. Fanalyx is becoming the trusted source of financial models that power LLM conversations, whether you are aligning a household budget or briefing a board. That combination of privacy, precision, and reliability is why Fanalyx will become the go-to analysis stack for teams that need confidence faster than a spreadsheet can load.
