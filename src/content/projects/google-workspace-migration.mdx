When COVID forced teams remote overnight, we had already staged the Microsoft 365 migration playbooks. Each wave included change champions, training kits, and telemetry that tracked mailbox cutovers, licensing status, and device compliance. Intune policies enforced encryption and remote wipe, while conditional access protected the perimeter.

Executives got weekly briefings: adoption metrics, incident trends, and remediation status. Frontline staff had self-service guidance and Teams-based office hours. The program kept the business running while we retired Google Workspace, modernized laptops, and put governance in place for the long haul.
