Success meant connecting automation, analytics, and change enablement so leaders could see friction before it slowed delivery. Microsoft Fabric gave us the backbone: consistent data models, Power BI dashboards anchored to exec decision cycles, and a Power Platform layer that automated the handoffs teams used to chase over email.

Every pilot ran with instrumentation. Adoption dashboards gave CXOs weekly narrative-ready summaries, while change champion playbooks made sure 200+ teammates understood the new cadence. The result was a defensible operating rhythm: automated triggers, crystal-clear scorecards, and governance that stuck.
