Ferment App helps hobbyists and professionals manage recipes, schedules, and tasting notes from one native experience. SwiftUI components handle recipe creation and visualization, while SwiftData keeps entries consistent offline.

Firebase powers collaboration: shared batches, push notifications, and analytics. Automations remind users when to stir, vent, or taste. The result is a playful yet robust product that turns fermentation into a repeatable, shareable craft.
