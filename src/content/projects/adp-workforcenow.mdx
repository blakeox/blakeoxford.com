ADP Workforce Now became the connective tissue across HR, recruiting, and finance. Automated workflows cut down repetitive data entry, and integrations with Sage Intacct ensured payroll, expenses, and GL postings stayed aligned.

Power BI dashboards gave executives instant visibility: headcount, hiring velocity, turnover, and payroll allocations in one place. The organization replaced spreadsheet chaos with a system people trusted.
