My Approach

I tackle projects through a holistic lens: process, people, and technology. Here's the structured methodology I follow to ensure success.

See how this methodology drives real results in the projects below
Step 1

Discovery & Empathy

Uncover true pain points and human factors.

Conduct stakeholder interviews and shadow frontline staff to uncover true pain points and human factors that drive successful implementations.

Step 2

Process Mapping

Document workflows before selecting tools.

Use BPMN and workshops to document workflows, handoffs, and responsibilities before selecting tools. This ensures technology serves the process, not the other way around.

Step 3

Pre-Sales Preparation

Align vendor evaluations with needs.

Prepare detailed SOPs, workflows, and risk assessments to align vendor evaluations with organizational needs and ensure successful procurement decisions.

Step 4

Design Thinking & Iteration

Prototype, gather feedback, refine.

Prototype solutions rapidly, gather user feedback, and refine in agile sprints to balance critique with encouragement and drive continuous improvement.

Step 5

Technical Implementation

Build scalable, compliant systems.

Integrate best-in-class platforms and custom scripts—leveraging SQL, Python, or LLMs—to build scalable, compliant systems that grow with your organization.

Step 6

Governance & Continuous Improvement

Evolve solutions, empower teams.

Establish reviews, training, and documentation to evolve solutions over years and empower teams to maintain and enhance systems independently.
